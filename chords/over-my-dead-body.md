# Over My Dead Body — Drake

**Original key: A♭ major · ~86 BPM**

## The loop (repeats the whole song)

**D♭ → A♭ → E♭m → E♭**

| Chord | Right hand | Bass (left hand) |
|-------|------------|------------------|
| D♭ | D♭–F–A♭ | G♭ |
| A♭ | C–D♭–F–A♭ | A♭ |
| E♭m | A♭–C–E♭ | D♭ |
| E♭ | G♭–B♭–D♭–F | E♭ |

## Easier version (all white-key friendly)

**Fmaj7 → G → Cmaj7 → Dm7**
