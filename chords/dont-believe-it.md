## How it is played

Short, stabby, syncopated hits — beat 1, the "and" of 2, beat 4 — over a
four-on-the-floor pulse. Nothing held. Before a new section the drums drop
out under a white-noise riser, then a crash lands with the groove.

Effects on the piano: dotted-eighth delay + reverb; the bass pumps
(sidechain dip on every kick).
