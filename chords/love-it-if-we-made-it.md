# Love It If We Made It — The 1975

**Key: E major · Tempo: 90 BPM · 4/4**
Verified from the official Hal Leonard sheet music (Musicnotes) + the Ultimate Guitar official tab; Hooktheory agrees on 90 BPM.

## The chords

The whole song rides on two chords, then lifts:

- **Verse / Bridge:** A ↔ B (palm-muted drive)
- **Chorus:** A ↔ B, bigger
- **Chorus lift + Outro:** C♯m → A → B (the "tell me something I didn't know" line lands on C♯m)

## Piano voicings

| Chord | Left hand | Right hand | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| A (Asus2) | A | E – A – B | The most-used chord in the song is really Asus2 — open and ringing |
| B | B | F♯ – B – E♭(D♯) | B major |
| C♯m | C♯ | E – G♯ – C♯ | The emotional dip before the lift |

## Shape of the song

- **Intro (~24s):** quiet staccato chords, sparkle synths and chimes, building to a glissando into the verse
- **Verses:** A ↔ B chugging under the stream-of-consciousness vocal
- **Choruses:** same chords, everything bigger; second chorus adds funk guitar bursts
- **Ending:** C♯m → A → B repeats, closing with staccato "ticking clock" strings
